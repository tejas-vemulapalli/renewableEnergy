Fossil Fuels

Generating energy with fossil fuels requires burning coal and oil in a furnace to create thermal energy. Water is poured into the furnace which makes high pressure steam. This steam is forced in to a turbine chamber that a generator is connected to. While this seems efficient, it creates massive amounts of greenhouse gasses. 



# Why Should We Switch To An Alternative



**Fossil fuels are the main contributor to climate change. Burning them releases greenhouse gases, primarily carbon dioxide, which trap heat in the atmosphere. This rising global temperature melts polar ice caps and increases sea levels. Additionally, the smoke emissions fill the air with harmful air pollutants and soot, which degrade air quality and further accelerate warming.**



**There are many alternatives to fossil fuels; solar, wind, geothermal and hydro-electric. These are called renewable energy. Renewable energy is energy harvested from natural sources that can constantly be replenished within a human lifespan.Using these could deeply help climate change.** 



**If you cannot afford these solutions you could simply turn off lights when you are not using them or turning off the heater when you are out of the house.**



# Renewable Energy



There are four main types of renewable energy: solar, wind, hydro, and geothermal. These four types rely on light, kinetic energy and heat. Out of the four sources, hydropower is the most efficient, converting 90% of kinetic energy into usable power. However, the most commonly used sources of energy are fossil fuels, which burn carbon-based materials, boosting greenhouse gas emissions and climate change. About 40% of the world's energy comes from renewable sources. 5% is solar, 10% is wind, and 17% is hydro. This shows how effective renewable energy can be at maintaining a clean and sustainable environment.

It is our responsibility to adopt renewable energy sources and the government can help this process by lowering the prices of solar panels and providing rebates for grid generation, to people who install solar panels on their home or business. The Australian Government has a goal for renewable energy - to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 42% by 2030. They plan to do this by converting things such as, transportation and large scale buildings to more energy efficient electrical models. This will also improve energy performance. In addition to this, adding more renewable implants across the country can generate new jobs, such as maintenance and installation, which can help stabilise economic structures. This will help the government adopt independence and save money for future funding.

The use of fossil fuels impacts many parts of the world but the most affected zone in Asia. This area consumes 60% of the world's coal, with countries including South Korea and Japan whom heavily rely on imported coal. 20% of the world's coal comes through the Strait of Hormuz which is currently closed due to the recent conflict between Iran and the USA. This forced countries to delay shutdown of coal fired plants and max the input on coal fired plants.

Using fossil fuels is bad for the environment because the mining and drilling for coal destroy landscapes and ecosystems endangering animals. Also burning coal releases carbon dioxide which gets trapped in the atmosphere creating a heat blanket around the earth.

Renewable energy still comes with downsides if mass produced. For instance, solar panels affect desert biomes by clearing land and disturbing desert water balance. Using wind turbines in forests can also clear land and blocks migration paths. Using hydropower with dams can affect natural water flow and block migration paths as well. In rare circumstances, geothermal energy can lead to acid rain by releasing hydrogen atoms into a moist atmosphere.
