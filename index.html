Fossil Fuels & Climate Change

Burning fossil fuels releases greenhouse gases, trapping heat and accelerating climate change. Mining destroys landscapes and ecosystems.

Rising temperatures melt polar ice caps, raise sea levels, and worsen air pollution.

Global Coal Use

Asia consumes 60% of the world's coal. South Korea and Japan rely heavily on imports.

20% of global coal passes through the Strait of Hormuz, currently closed due to conflict.

Australian Government Goals

Australia aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 42% by 2030.

Plans include converting transportation and large buildings to efficient electrical models.

Renewable expansion creates jobs in installation and maintenance.

Everyday Actions

If renewable solutions are unaffordable, simple actions like turning off lights or heaters help reduce energy use.
